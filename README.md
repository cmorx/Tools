### In case you want a/an different…

Wrap your own images around an object and watch them turn. Thirteen objects — a bagel,
an egg, an apple, a cleaver, a traffic cone, a rubber duck, an office chair — six ways to
project an image onto them, and a cut-out mode that carves the form by luminance.
Exports a PNG, an MP4, or a standalone page you can embed anywhere.

**→ [cmorx.github.io/Tools/incase](https://cmorx.github.io/Tools/incase/)**

---

### Wander Type

A typeface that writes itself by walking. Letterforms drift, wander and trail
according to a handful of parameters. Exports SVG and PNG.

**→ [cmorx.github.io/Tools/wander-type](https://cmorx.github.io/Tools/wander-type/)**

---

### Concrete Poem

A camera that writes. Text flows along the contours it finds in a live feed, a video
or a still, and the image is pushed toward a damp, overexposed, photocopied surface.

**→ [cmorx.github.io/Tools/concrete-camera](https://cmorx.github.io/Tools/concrete-camera/)**

---

Built with vanilla JavaScript. Best in Chrome — video export uses WebCodecs.
